The Domain Experts and Contributors records follow the published order on the
project-owned TianGong Wix site. Expert portraits are checked-in 480 × 480 WebP
derivatives of the corresponding Wix media. Contributor contact details were not
copied into the Portal directory.

Partner institutions follow the workbook row order. Logo files are checked-in
as transparent WebP derivatives unless a cleaner public SVG or high-resolution
transparent PNG is available. The 58 matching workbook attachments were
supplemented with marks retrieved from the following institutional or
institution-derived public sources on 2026-09-09:
