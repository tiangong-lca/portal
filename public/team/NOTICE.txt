These portraits and the corresponding public profile fields were copied from the
project-owned TianGong Wix site for use on the TianGong LCA Portal. The checked-in
WebP files are 804 × 752 optimized copies. Member names, roles, institutions and
published email addresses are recorded in src/features/team/team-data.ts and the
localized Portal dictionaries.

The 23 PNG files in cutouts/ are deterministic foreground masks generated
from the corresponding source portraits with rembg u2net_human_seg. The process
only replaces background pixels with alpha transparency and does not synthesize
or redraw faces, clothing, poses or body geometry. They are currently used by
the Storybook TeamEnsemble design study.
