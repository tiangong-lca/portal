Tiangong LCA lifecycle sculpture, Portal Storybook exploration

lifecycle-models.glb
Authored geometry for this Portal exploration; no downloaded model or texture.
Editable Blender source and reproducible authoring functions are retained in
.storybook/brand-exploration/modeling/. The live sculpture uses this geometry,
procedural plates, nodes, lines and geographic points with runtime materials.

reference-dark.png / reference-light.png
User-selected visual references generated with OpenAI ImageGen during the
Portal brand exploration. Original boards are retained unchanged. Only explicit
comparison stories display the sculpture area with a CSS crop. These images
are never sampled by the live scene. Any people, records or counters elsewhere
on the boards are illustrative placeholders.
Source dimensions, crop and SHA-256 receipts are in
.storybook/brand-exploration/artwork.json.
